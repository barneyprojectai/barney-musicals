Theory

Scales, intervals and the circle of fifths

Pick a key or an interval and the keyboard shows and plays it.

12 tones and 7 tones

All music uses the same 12 notes. A scale picks 7 of them by a pattern of whole (W) and half (H) steps.

C major

Intervals

An interval is the distance between two notes. Count both notes: C to E is C (1), D (2), E (3), a 3rd.

up to
Major 3rd

Circle of fifths

Going clockwise, each key is a 5th higher and adds one sharp. Going anticlockwise adds one flat. The two keys beside any key are its IV and V chords.
